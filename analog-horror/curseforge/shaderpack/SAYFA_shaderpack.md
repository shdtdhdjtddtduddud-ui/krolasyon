## A shaderpack that looks like a tape you found in an attic.

**Hatman Analog** was made for the **The Hatman** horror mod, but works in any world.

### ✨ Features
- **Soft sun & moon shadows** with rotated PCF filtering
- **Warm, flickering candle and lamp light**; cold, dim moonlight — nights are genuinely dark
- **Glowing light sources**: torches, lanterns, campfires, candles and the mod's sconces and lamps bloom; the mod's old CRT television flickers with static
- **Fog that thickens at night** and an animated **ground mist** that creeps around your feet
- **Bloom**, ACES tonemapping and cinematic **color grades**: Neutral, Analog, Sepia Found Footage, Bleach Bypass
- **Analog camera** (all optional): VHS tape wobble & color bleed, scanlines, film grain, vignette, a blinking **REC** light
- **Hatman Shadow Form**: the Hatman swallows light and only shows a cold rim
- Waving grass and plants

### ⚙️ Options
Every effect can be tuned or switched off in **Video Settings → Shader Packs → Shader Options**. Presets: **Low · Medium · High · Found Tape**. The options menu is in **English** and **Türkçe**.

### 📦 Requirements
| Minecraft | Loader | Mods |
|---|---|---|
| 1.20.1 Forge | Forge 47 | **Oculus** + **Embeddium** *(tested)* |
| Fabric / Quilt | — | **Iris** + Sodium |
| Vanilla / OptiFine | — | **OptiFine** |

> Tested in-game with Oculus 1.8.0 + Embeddium 0.3.31 on Forge 1.20.1. Forge 1.21.1 currently has no maintained Iris port.

### 📥 Installation
1. Install Oculus + Embeddium (Forge) or Iris (Fabric) or OptiFine.
2. Put `HatmanAnalog.zip` into `.minecraft/shaderpacks` — **don't unzip it**.
3. In game: Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs → **HatmanAnalog**.
