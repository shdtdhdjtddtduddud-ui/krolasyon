## Promptlar

Ortak stil eki (her promptun sonuna): *Photorealistic, IMAX documentary quality, volumetric light, anamorphic flares, ultra-detailed, no on-screen text.*

1. **Kara delik** — SHOT 1 (0-3s, HOOK): extreme fast push-in toward a pitch-black sphere ringed by a blazing orange accretion disk, light bending around it, sudden bass hit, deep Turkish narrator: "Buraya düşersen, spagetti olursun." SHOT 2 (3-7s): astronaut silhouette falling feet-first, body stretching like a noodle, gravitational lensing warps stars. SHOT 3 (7-10s): slow orbit around the glowing photon ring, orchestral swell, narrator: "Bilim buna spagettileşme diyor."
2. **Üçlü nokta** — SHOT 1 (0-3s, HOOK): macro of water in a vacuum chamber violently boiling AND freezing at once, ice shooting across bubbles, sharp crack, narrator: "Bu su aynı anda hem kaynıyor hem donuyor." SHOT 2: fractal ice crystals growing between rising bubbles, cold blue rim light. SHOT 3: pull-back to dark lab, pressure gauge dropping, narrator: "Buna üçlü nokta denir."
3. **Yıldız tozu** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): extreme close-up of a human eye, iris dissolves into a swirling galaxy, deep boom, narrator: "Vücudundaki atomlar bir yıldızın içinde doğdu." SHOT 2: massive star collapses into a supernova, glowing particles fly at camera. SHOT 3: stardust assembles into a human hand silhouette, narrator: "Sen kelimenin tam anlamıyla yıldız tozusun."
4. **Tardigrad** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): tiny eight-legged water bear floating in black space with Earth behind, silence then heartbeat, narrator: "Bu canlı uzay boşluğunda hayatta kalabiliyor." SHOT 2: microscope macro of it curling into a dried 'tun', frost and radiation glow. SHOT 3: a water drop revives it on green moss, narrator: "Su ayısı: dünyanın en dayanıklı hayvanı."
5. **Mantis karidesi** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): rainbow mantis shrimp strikes a snail shell, shell SHATTERS with a flash and underwater crack, narrator: "Bu karides mermi hızında yumruk atıyor." SHOT 2: extreme slow motion, cavitation bubble collapses emitting a tiny flash. SHOT 3: close-up of iridescent eyes on a coral reef, narrator: "Darbesi suyu bile kaynatıyor."
6. **Yıldırım** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): lightning bolt strikes directly toward camera in slow motion over a dark city, blinding white flash, thunder crack, narrator: "Bu ışık, Güneş'in yüzeyinden 5 kat daha sıcak." SHOT 2: thermal-camera style view of the plasma channel glowing white-hot, air exploding outward. SHOT 3: side-by-side visual of the Sun's surface and the bolt, narrator: "Yaklaşık 30.000 derece."
7. **Nötron yıldızı** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): a silver teaspoon holding a tiny glowing blue-white ball of matter crashes through a wooden table and floor, deep impact, narrator: "Bu kaşık dolusu madde bir dağdan ağır." SHOT 2: a spinning neutron star in space with pulsar beams sweeping like a lighthouse. SHOT 3: the spoon next to a mountain on a giant balance scale, mountain lifted up, narrator: "Bir çay kaşığı: milyarlarca ton."
8. **Ahtapot** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): an octopus bursts out of a coral cave straight at camera, changing color instantly, narrator: "Bu hayvanın üç kalbi ve mavi kanı var." SHOT 2: x-ray style glowing visualization of three beating hearts pumping blue blood. SHOT 3: each tentacle moving independently solving a puzzle, narrator: "Ve dokuz beyni."
9. **Güneş ışığı** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): a person looks at a sunset, the Sun suddenly vanishes and a giant "8:20" timer-like glow pulse (no text) ripples across the sky, deep whoosh, narrator: "Şu an gördüğün Güneş, 8 dakika önceki Güneş." SHOT 2: a beam of light racing from the Sun across space past Mercury and Venus toward Earth. SHOT 3: the light hits the person's eye in macro, narrator: "Işık bile beklemek zorunda."
10. **Venüs** — SHOT 1 (HOOK): fast dive through thick yellow acid clouds onto Venus' volcanic surface, rumble, narrator: "Bu gezegende bir gün, bir yıldan uzun." SHOT 2: time-lapse of Venus rotating extremely slowly backward while orbiting the Sun. SHOT 3: lava plains glowing under an orange sky, narrator: "Venüs: 243 gün dönüş, 225 gün yörünge."
