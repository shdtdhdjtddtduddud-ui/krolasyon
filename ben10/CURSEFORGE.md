## Description (CurseForge editor → switch to Markdown, then paste)

```markdown
# Ben 10: Omnitrix Unleashed

**It's hero time!** Strap on the Omnitrix, spin the dial and slam it down to become one of **9 iconic aliens**. Every alien has its own body, size, stats and fighting style - with **4 special abilities**, a **unique guard** and passive powers. No decoration: every power is a real weapon, and many of them reshape the world around you.

## ✨ Features

- **Omnitrix dial** - rotating selection wheel with alien portraits and ability previews
- **9 playable aliens** with custom models, textures and glowing details
- **36 abilities + 9 guards** with animations, particles, sounds and camera shake
- **Smooth animations** - walking, sprinting, flying, ghost floating, rolling, punching
- **World interaction** - melt ice, freeze lakes, turn lava into obsidian, bake sand into glass, raise crystal walls, rip boulders from the ground, shatter glass with sound waves
- **Unique stats** - health, armour, speed, strength, reach and hitbox size per alien
- **Omnitrix timer** - the watch beeps, times out and recharges (configurable)
- **HUD** - portrait, time bar, abilities and cooldowns
- **Multiplayer ready** - everyone sees your alien form and effects

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **G** / right-click Omnitrix | Open the dial, click again (or Enter) to transform |
| **Z / X / C / V** | Ability 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 |
| **R** (hold) | Guard |
| **Left click** | Alien melee |

Rebind in *Options → Controls → Ben 10 Omnitrix*.

## 👽 Aliens

| Alien | Abilities | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Heatblast** | Fireball · Flamethrower · Supernova · Fire Geysers | Fire Shield (burns arrows & attackers) |
| 💪 **Four Arms** | Ground Slam · Thunder Clap · Mighty Leap · Grab & Throw | Four-Arm Block |
| 💎 **Diamondhead** | Crystal Shards · Crystal Spikes · Crystal Wall · Crystal Storm | Crystal Shield (reflects projectiles) |
| ⚡ **XLR8** | Speed Dash · Cyclone · Blitz Combo · Overdrive | Evasion |
| 🟢 **Goop** | Acid Spit · Slime Whip · Engulf · Goo Wave | Liquid Body (projectiles pass through) |
| 🛡️ **Cannonbolt** | Rolling Charge · Cannonball Launch · Bounce Bomb · Ricochet | Armoured Shell (90% block) |
| ❄️ **Big Chill** | Ice Breath · Ice Prison · Ice Spear · Blizzard | Wing Cloak (freezes attackers) |
| 🔊 **Ultimate Echo Echo** | Sonic Discs · Sonic Beam · Disc Barrage · Sonic Doom | Disc Wall |
| 👻 **Ghostfreak** | Shadow Claw · Fear Gaze · Possession · Phantom Form | Ghostly Evasion (50% dodge) |

**Passives:** Heatblast flies and is fire-immune (but water hurts him) · Four Arms jumps very high · Diamondhead has huge armour · XLR8 runs on water · Goop takes no fall damage and is poison-immune · Cannonbolt is almost impossible to knock back · Big Chill flies and freezes water beneath him · Echo Echo hovers · Ghostfreak flies and sees in the dark.

## 📦 Getting the Omnitrix

Creative tab **Ben 10 Omnitrix**, or `/give @s krolben10:omnitrix`

## ⚙️ Config

`config/krolben10-common.toml` - transformation time (default 300 s), recharge (20 s), damage multiplier, cooldown multiplier, and `abilityGriefing` to stop abilities from changing blocks.

> Heatblast can set grass and leaves on fire - disable `abilityGriefing` or use `/gamerule doFireTick false` if you don't want spreading fire.

## 📋 Requirements

Minecraft **1.20.1** · **Forge 47.x** · install on **client and server**
```
