## Description
```markdown
# Cille: Marble Battles

**The legendary Cilles from the Turkish cartoon *Cille* come to Minecraft!** Throw your marble, smash it into your rival's marble and the Cilles burst out to fight. Every Cille has its own 3D model, animations, sounds and the techniques from the show.

> ⚠️ This is an unofficial, fan-made mod. Cille and its characters belong to their respective owners; this mod is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

## 🔮 Marbles
- **Right click to throw a Cille marble.** It bounces and rolls like a real marble, with a tiny Cille turning inside the glass.
- **When two marbles collide** both burst and the Cilles appear face to face, ready to fight.
- **Cille bond:** the first player to throw a marble becomes that Cille's **Cahan**. Nobody else can open the marble, and the Cille obeys only its Cahan.
- A defeated Cille never dies – it **retreats into its marble** and rests for a while.
- Wild Cilles (spawn eggs) fight like bosses. Beat one and it drops its empty marble, so you can capture it.

## 🎮 Battle commands
| Key | Command |
|---|---|
| **R** | Command menu (orders + techniques) |
| **V** | Attack the target you are looking at |
| **G** | Hold fire (passive) |
| **H** | Defend |
| **B** | Retreat |
| **X** | Go where you are looking |
| **N** | Switch the Cille you command |
| **ALT + 1…9, 0** | Techniques of the selected Cille |

Right click your Cille: stay / follow. Shift + right click: call it back into its marble. Every key can be rebound in Options → Controls → Cille.

## 🐾 The Cilles

### Kabkaba – the dark Cille
Black Hole · Teleport Strike · Energy Drain · Reflect · Shadow Attack · Dark Guard · Ground Slam
*+ dodges projectiles, counter-attacks instantly*

### Behmud – the flying cosmic whale
Ice Beam · Whirlpool · Sound Wave · Exploding Fish · Torpedo Fire · Quick Attack · Waterspout · Ice Shield · Quick Defence · Erkemas Clamp · Sealed Power · Space Power

### Şarkan – the sand and rock warrior
Sand Vortex · Atlas Attack · Deveran Attack · Desert Storm · Sand Sea · Sand Shield · Rock Throw · Sand Walls
*+ high endurance, crushing fists*

### Nirumend – the giant earth mammoth
Bumehen Attack · Ajirak Attack · Bone Head Bash · Hard Rock Strike · Electric Shield · Dust Cloud · Ground Stomp
*+ enormous strength, high endurance*

## 🌍 It changes the battlefield
Craters, blocks torn out of the ground, frozen water, sand walls, earth swept up by waterspouts… Terrain damage follows the **`mobGriefing`** game rule – turn it off and the Cilles leave blocks alone.

## 📦 Requirements
- Minecraft **1.20.1**
- Forge **47.x**
- Works in singleplayer and on servers (install it on both the server and the client).
```
