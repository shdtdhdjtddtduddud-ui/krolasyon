# Boss Mod (Forge 1.20.1)

## Azrakor (Hell dimension)

* Find a ruined **hellgate** in the overworld (generates naturally) or craft a frame of *Hellgate Stone* and strike it with a **Hell Spark** (blaze powder + hellsteel nugget + flint).
* Azrakor holds five houses (Ember, Bone, Blood, Shadow, Rot) with 39 creature types and bosses. Talk to envoys at outposts (right click), do their hunts, then swear an oath to a ruler or break them.
* Right-click the **Chronicle** (book + ember shard) for the story and your standing.
* Collect three house sigils, right-click the sealed wall of the Throne Hall to wake the Ash Sovereign.
* Hellsteel swords and spell tomes use mana (bar above the hotbar); right click to cast.
